Computer-based English exam to determine university-level readiness. Recognized worldwide for student visas and university admissions.

Scoring Breakdown

PTE Scoring Parameters & CLB Mapping

PTE scoring is a unique combination of AI scoring and human expertise, reducing the risk of bias. Score ranges on a scale of 10 - 90.

1. Enabling Skills

Language skills that support communication and influence your communicative skills scores:

Grammar

Correct use of tense, sentence structure, syntax.

Impacts Writing & Speaking
Oral Fluency

Smooth, natural speech, minimal hesitation.

Impacts Speaking
Pronunciation

Clear, intelligible pronunciation.

Impacts Speaking
Spelling

Correct spelling in writing.

Impacts Writing
Vocabulary

Range and accuracy of words.

Impacts Writing & Speaking
Written Discourse

Coherence, paragraphing, organization.

Impacts Writing

2. Communicative Skills

Speaking: Fluency, pronunciation & language use.
Writing: Written discourse, grammar & spelling.
Reading: Comprehension & text processing.
Listening: Understanding spoken English.
