Required Documentation

Application & Visa Checklist

Keep these mandatory documents organized for an error-free, streamlined application and visa approval.

📋

Documents for Application

  • ✓ 10th Marksheet & Passing Certificate
  • ✓ 12th Marksheet & Passing Certificate
  • ✓ Graduation Individual Marksheets, Degree / Provisional Degree, Transcripts
  • ✓ English Proficiency Test: IELTS / PTE / TOEFL / Duolingo
  • ✓ Valid Passport (all pages)
  • ✓ Updated Resume / CV
  • ✓ Statement of Purpose (SOP)
  • ✓ 2 Academic Letters of Recommendation (LORs)
  • ✓ Work Experience Certificates & Internship Letters
🛂

Visa Document Checklist

  • ✓ Letter of Acceptance from the Irish College/University
  • ✓ Official Tuition Fee Receipt / Electronic Confirmation
  • ✓ Private Medical Insurance Cover
  • ✓ Police Clearance Certificate (PCC)
  • ✓ Birth Certificate in English
  • ✓ 2 Latest Passport Size Photographs
  • ✓ Current Passport & Full copies of all previous passports
  • ✓ Visa Application Form + Signed Financial Summary Form
💼

Financial Documents (Visa Proof)

  • ✓ Education Loan Sanction Letter (for fee payments)
  • ✓ Affidavit of Support from Sponsor(s)
  • ✓ Living expenses in Sponsor's 6-month-old Savings Account / FD / PF
  • ✓ ITR Requirement: ₹8 Lacs+ annual income
  • Salaried Sponsor: 6 months bank statement, last 3 years ITR, 6 months salary slips, Job ID, Form 16A, Appointment & Appraisal letter.
  • Business Sponsor: Business bank statement, last 3 years ITR with tax computation, registration/MOA. ITR ₹8 Lac+ required.
