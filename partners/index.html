Why Study Abroad Consulting is a Smart Business Move

Low Entry Barrier

Start lean with minimal infrastructure—solo consultant or small team. Scale to franchise model as deal flow grows. No heavy capital investment required.

High-Ticket Revenue

Each student represents multiple monetization points—counselling, applications, test prep, loans, forex—leading to strong lifetime value.

Compounding Growth

Every successful placement builds testimonials and word-of-mouth. Alumni relationships create referral loops, making lead generation cheaper over time.

Long-Term Macro Tailwind

Ride a structural demand wave, not a fad. Outbound flows remain high with policy support, perfect for building a compounding consultancy brand.
